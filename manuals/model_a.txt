# Model A -- small house. 16x16 baseplate, two layers, 12 steps.
# DRAFT: a placeholder design until the team fixes Model A against the bricks
# actually in hand (plan section 12). Coordinates: studs from the bottom-left corner.

1. Place a red 2x4 brick lengthwise along the bottom edge, in the bottom-left corner.
2. Place a second red 2x4 brick lengthwise to the right of the first, completing the front wall.
3. Place a blue 2x2 brick directly behind the left end of the front wall.
4. Place a blue 2x2 brick directly behind the right end of the front wall.
5. Place a white 1x2 brick crosswise just behind the middle of the left red brick, as the doorstep.
6. Place a yellow 1x4 brick lengthwise behind the left blue brick, forming the left half of the back wall.
7. Place a yellow 1x4 brick lengthwise to the right of it, completing the back wall.
8. Stack a green 2x4 brick exactly on top of the left red brick.
9. Stack a green 2x4 brick exactly on top of the right red brick.
10. Stack a black 2x2 brick exactly on top of the left blue brick.
11. Stack a black 2x2 brick exactly on top of the right blue brick.
12. Bridge the join in the back wall with a white 1x2 brick laid crosswise over both yellow bricks.
